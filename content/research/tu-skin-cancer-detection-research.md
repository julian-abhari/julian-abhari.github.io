- Conducted research in computer vision with a focus on medical image analysis for skin cancer detection.
- Developed a machine learning model to identify potentially cancerous moles from patient-provided images.
- Built and deployed an iOS application enabling users to scan and assess moles for signs of skin cancer.
- Integrated AI diagnostics into a user-friendly mobile interface to support early intervention and self-screening.
