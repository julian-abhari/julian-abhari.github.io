- Developed ML models using TensorFlow and PyTorch to predict hand movements from motor cortex signals.
- Implemented and evaluated Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Random Forests, and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) algorithms.
- Designed and tested an ensemble learning approach to improve prediction accuracy across models.
- Contributed to a co-authored paper on model-selection voting for EEG data assessment (see [[ml-democracy-icmla22]]).
