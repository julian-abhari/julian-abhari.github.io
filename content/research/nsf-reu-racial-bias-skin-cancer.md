- Conducted research on domain adaptation to improve model generalization across diverse image datasets.
- Implemented CycleGAN to translate images between domains and reduce dataset bias.
- Adapted and aligned multiple datasets to support equitable model performance across varying skin tones.
- Trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image classification tasks.
- Integrated the end-to-end AI pipeline into a mobile application for real-world deployment.
- Authored and published a peer-reviewed paper detailing research findings and technical innovations (see [[mitigating-racial-biases-mobihoc23]]).
