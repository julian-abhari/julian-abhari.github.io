Founder and CTO of SkinCheck, leading the development of AI-driven solutions for early skin cancer detection.

- Spearheaded the creation of a mobile app that has provided timely dermatological care to over 170,000 patients globally.
- Conducted pioneering research in computer vision, resulting in peer-reviewed publications in top-tier computer science venues.
- Invented the first visual AI skin cancer detector designed for accuracy across all skin tones; patent-pending.
- Drives product innovation at the intersection of healthcare and technology to democratize access to expert dermatological assessment.
- Oversees end-to-end software development, AI model deployment, and technical strategy for a global digital health platform.
- Leads engineering and development for the SkinCheck tele-dermatology platform, SC Connect.
