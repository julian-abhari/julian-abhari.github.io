Before my app became my startup company, it was my high-school passion project that I was developing to help my mom with her own skin cancer struggles.

Skin Check is a free assistive tool to allow anyone to check their moles for skin cancer, track their moles over time, and compare their moles to an index of different cancerous and non-cancerous mole types.

According to the Skin Cancer Foundation, two people die every hour from skin-cancer in the U.S. alone. The hope for my app is to bring down this alarming statistic by letting users know if their moles are concerning and recommending them to seek treatment.

I have spent four years working on this app, the first version was released just before my senior year of high school in 2019, and then I picked it back up in 2021 during the end of my Freshman year in college where I worked on it thanks to the Tulsa Undergraduate Research Challenge (TURC) and released it during my Sophomore year of college.
