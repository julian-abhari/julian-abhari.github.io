Art always serves as a reflection of the artist, but also as a reflection of the viewers, allowing them to embark on a journey of self-discovery through the worlds of the artist. To me, abstract art is the best way you can understand yourself because, as you search for the artist's meaning, you're left with your own meaning, which in turn becomes you. In my artworks, I try to recreate this sense of self-discovery through surreal and abstract visuals that try to fascinate the viewer as they search for themselves. I kept trying to prove that programming was so much more than just lines of code, and should definitely be considered a form of art. Through this frustration, I tried to make my art as abstract and strange as I could, in order to captivate the viewer.
