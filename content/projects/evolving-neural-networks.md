During the summer between my Sophomore and Junior year of high-school I began work on an AI Algorithm I'd always been wanting to make: Neural Evolution of Augmented Topologies. I remember back in eighth grade when I first saw a YouTube video of someone training an AI to play Super Mario. I thought it was the coolest thing ever, and I was convinced this was the algorithm that could create self-aware AI. Finally, once I knew enough about AI to make neural networks from scratch in Java, I used my custom game engine to build a virtual environment that mimicked the game "Flappy Bird". Using this environment, I would evolve a population of AI to beat Flappy Bird. After a month or so, I had finally done it.
