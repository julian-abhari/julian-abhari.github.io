I carved a box to hold a Linux computer I built that runs a program I wrote to display a photo in a dynamic and artistic way, such that it is continuously moving and shows beauty. The photo is of me and my girlfriend portrayed as little dots that flee from wind and gravitate back towards each other, symbolizing our strength and care for each other through our relationship.
