My four year honors program at The University of Tulsa culminated in the creation of beautiful artworks generated through computer programs I built to encapsulate the cosmic idea of emergence, how beautifully complex systems come into view through slight interaction changes within the smallest atoms that make up the system.

Amazingly, these artworks are put on permanent display at The University of Tulsa's Tandy School of Computer Science in Rayzor Hall.

Take a look at the artworks and a brief description of the systems I built to emerge them here.
