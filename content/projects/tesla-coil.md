After spending many years dreaming about a world where Tesla's wireless transmission of energy technology could be used to send solar panels into space and have them beam energy down to Earth, creating a green and easily accessible source of energy, I spent the end of my Sophomore year and the beginning of my Junior year of high-school finally creating a Tesla Coil with my Physics teacher.

This was quite an enormous task, as it forced me to learn the basics of electricity, modifying a high-voltage transformer, building high-voltage capacitors, winding the primary and secondary coils, learning about spark gaps, and finally, wiring everything together and tuning it.
