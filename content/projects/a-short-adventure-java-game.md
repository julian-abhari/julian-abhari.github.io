Expanded upon the [[electroblast-java-game]] engine to add in more story-driven elements, such as NPCs that you can talk to and adaptive music. Development continued over the years, most recently adding tuned 2D physics and mobile tap support. The engine was later ported — rendering, input, and resource loading — to WebAssembly via TeaVM, becoming the engine that powers this portfolio site itself.

Publicly titled "A Short Adventure" rather than its working/internal name to keep a family member's name private.
